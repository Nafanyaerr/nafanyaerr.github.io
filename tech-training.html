Technician Training Platform

An in-house platform for training and certifying WeFix technicians.

Overview

Context

WeFix used a third-party learning management system. As its team grew, the company needed more control over training quality and the flexibility to adapt courses to its own processes.

My role

I created the design system and interaction patterns. I also joined design workshops and reviewed other designers’ work to keep the sections we developed in parallel consistent.

01. The challenge

The existing system recorded course completions. It told the training team little about what a technician had learned or could do independently on the job.

The company needed a consistent way to train technicians and decide who was ready for certification.

Technicians needed to see which courses were available, how far they had progressed and what to do next.

02. Linking training to assessment

Working with users

I mapped how the training team created and assigned courses, how technicians moved through the programme and what the team could see about their progress.

I met with the project stakeholder and the training team to find out what evidence they needed to spot gaps in a technician’s knowledge and decide when to certify them.

Assessment and certification

The hypothesis was that assessing knowledge separately from course completion, then using the results to decide what came next, would make technician training more consistent.

The team designed tests and an internal certification process. Assessment results and each technician’s qualification level determined which advanced courses they could take.

At certain stages, camera and location data gave the team additional information about testing conditions. Separate tests assessed what technicians had learned.

03. Making the training programme easier to follow

Working with users

I asked technicians how they found the course they needed, how they knew where they were in the programme and how they chose their next step.

The hypothesis was that a consistent catalogue and clear course statuses would help technicians find their next step without asking for guidance.

Comparable products

I compared how other products organised courses, modules and lessons, guided learners through the programme, showed progress, tested knowledge and moved learners between stages. This helped me identify familiar patterns we could adapt for the platform.

Design and testing

I designed the course cards and filters, organised the modules and lessons, and defined how the platform showed progress. Technicians could follow a course from the catalogue into a lesson and see their next step.

We gave technicians access to a test version and asked whether they could find their current course, see which stages they had finished and work out how to continue. Their feedback helped us refine the information hierarchy on the course cards and the way we showed progress.

Results

WeFix could update its own training platform as its processes changed. The training team used each technician’s progress and assessment results to decide who was ready for certification.

Course progression
Technicians could see which courses were open to them, finish the lessons and take an assessment. Their results and qualification level determined which courses opened next.
Consistent assessment
As the team grew, WeFix could assess technicians against a common standard. The assessment results helped the company monitor the quality of its maintenance and repair services.
