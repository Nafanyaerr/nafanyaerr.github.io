About

Hi, I’m Nastya — a UX/UI designer with a background in graphic design. I enjoy getting to grips with new challenges and joining side projects where I can explore ideas and build something with other people.

I draw, make linocut prints, paint, and enjoy airbrushing and photography. These interests feed into my design work through colour, composition and attention to detail. I also love making things by hand and seeing the result off-screen.

Outside design, I’m into cars, video games and board games. Sometimes I notice interesting interface ideas while playing, but mostly I’m just there to relax. I love animals, I’m mad about dinosaurs, and I dream of finding a real fossil one day.
